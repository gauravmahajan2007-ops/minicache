MiniCache — System Architecture

Four small, independently-verified subsystems compose into a KV-cache retention/reuse policy core. Data flows top to bottom on a reuse hit; the same path, with allocate + write instead of acquire + read, serves a miss.

The TTL cache MiniCache is the foundation the other three subsystems build on. Full specification, design, and per-subsystem reports live in SPEC_*.md, DESIGN_*.md, and REPORT_*.md.
